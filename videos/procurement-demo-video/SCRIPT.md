# Silent Demo Script

This video intentionally contains no narration or audio. Each beat leaves enough time for a presenter to add voiceover later.

1. High traffic and budget optimization: enter a NZD 1000 request, then hold on the optimized procurement report.
2. Christmas promotion readiness: show a promotion-driven request, then hold on the seasonal purchase decision.
3. Low budget stockout prevention: show a NZD 300 request, then hold on the budget-compliant priority plan.
4. Long shelf-life preference: show the shelf-life constraint, then hold on the filtered procurement report.
5. Fruit category focus: show the category-demand request, then hold on the focused plan.
6. No budget constraint: show an open replenishment request, then hold on the priority-ranked purchase plan.

Timing per scenario: request screen 2.8 seconds; procurement-plan screen 5.9 seconds; 0.4-second overlap between screens and 0.3-second overlap between scenarios.
